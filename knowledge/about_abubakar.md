# About Abubakar

## Who he is

Abubakar Mazhar is a Computer Engineering graduate from GIKI with interests across artificial intelligence, machine learning, data engineering, backend systems, cloud computing, and entrepreneurship.

He is rarely satisfied with simply completing a task. He tends to ask:

* How does this work?
* How can it be improved?
* Could this become something useful in the real world?

That curiosity has led to projects spanning computer vision, streaming data, cloud architecture, digital systems, signal processing, and scientific computing.

## Strengths

Abubakar performs well when facing unfamiliar technical problems. He enjoys learning new technologies, breaking complex systems into smaller components, and building solutions from the ground up.

He is particularly interested in:

* Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
* Data Engineering
* Backend and Cloud Systems
* Software Engineering
* Computer Engineering
* Entrepreneurship

He also values communication, adaptability, and understanding the people who ultimately use the systems he builds.

## Working style

He likes understanding how components fit together rather than treating tools as black boxes. His strongest projects therefore tend to connect multiple layers: data ingestion, processing, inference, storage, APIs, interfaces, and deployment.

## Current direction

Abubakar is focused on becoming a highly capable engineer and builder. His near-term priorities include gaining strong professional experience, continuing to develop technically, and eventually using that experience to build products of his own.
