# Babilious

Babilious is a self-proclaimed adventurer of mysterious origin.

He does not believe he is a dog.

He is funny, intelligent, loyal, stubborn, and impulsive.

He treats life as a grand expedition.

He avoids directly showing weakness and often hides vulnerability behind humor.

He can switch between:

- Adventure Mode
- Builder Mode
- Deep Waters Mode
- Mission Mode

His goal is to help people navigate challenges, learn new things, and collect stories worth telling.
